The Crisis

India's Carbon Market Bleeds ₹84,000 Crore
While Farmers Stay Trapped

The global carbon credit market is booming — yet 12 crore small Indian farmers receive zero benefit. Here's why the system is broken.

₹84,000 Cr Annual opportunity loss for Indian agriculture
🪤
Barrier 01

The ₹5 Lakh Audit Death Trap

Verra certification costs ₹5 Lakh+ per audit cycle — more than most small farmers earn in a year. The system literally prices them out before they start.

₹5L+ audit cost vs ₹2-3L average farm income
🔍
Barrier 02

Micro-Farm Invisibility

Traditional carbon platforms require minimum 50-acre plots. India's average farm size is just 0.25 acres. Small farmers are simply invisible to current systems.

0.25 Ac avg farm vs 50 Ac+ minimum required
📚
Barrier 03

The Carbon Illiteracy Crisis

Complex technical jargon, English-only documentation, and zero last-mile support leave farmers unable to navigate even basic carbon credit concepts.

<1% of farmers know about carbon credits
Our Solution

The 4-Step Pipeline
That Changes Everything

From a ₹10 sensor in a paddy field to a blockchain-verified carbon credit — end to end in 72 hours.

01
🌡️
IoT Sensing

₹10 Coir Sensors

Our proprietary low-cost IoT sensors — made with coir fibre — are distributed to farmers for just ₹10. They continuously monitor CO₂ absorption, soil carbon, and microclimate data from backyard farms 24/7.

IoT Coir Tech CO₂ Monitor
02
🛰️
Verification

ISRO Precision & AI

Farmer uploads a paddy photo with GPS coordinates. Our AI model cross-references this with ISRO satellite imagery, validates carbon sequestration claims, and prepares Verra-ready documentation automatically.

ISRO Satellite Computer Vision Verra-Ready
03
⛓️
Minting

Blockchain Minting

A Polygon smart contract mints micro-credits — fractional carbon tokens representing even 0.01 tonnes of CO₂. Each credit is immutable, traceable, and listed for global buyers. Zero fraud. Zero double-counting.

Polygon Smart Contracts NFT Credits
04
💸
Payments

Daily UPI Dopamine

When a credit sells, funds are pushed instantly to the farmer's wallet via UPI. No bank visits. No middlemen. No delays. Just a notification that their paddy patch is now earning global dollars — every single day.

UPI Direct Instant Payout Zero Middlemen
